The layer before the marketplace

You create the recommendation.
They handle the purchase.

Fortune Finds is your preparation and learning workspace. It is not a marketplace and does not sell physical products.

Research & create→Publish your content→Audience follows your link→External marketplace purchase

Any eligible commission follows the external program’s rules. Bring available reports back to Fortune Finds to understand the results and plan your next test.

Amazon, eBay, and Etsy are examples of external affiliate ecosystems. No official partnership is claimed.
